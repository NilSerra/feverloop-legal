Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

FeverLoop ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our automation application collects, uses, and safeguards information when connecting with third-party services like TikTok.

1. Information We Collect

FeverLoop accesses only the minimum necessary information required to perform video publishing operations on behalf of authorized channel owners:

  • Basic account identification (such as display name and channel handle) to confirm authorized access.
  • OAuth access tokens securely stored locally on our dedicated server for automated publishing tasks.

We do not collect personal contact details, payment information, or user telemetry.

2. How Information is Used

Any data or tokens obtained through the TikTok API or YouTube API are used solely for:

  • Authenticating video uploads initiated by the channel owner.
  • Scheduling and publishing original media loops directly to the authorized account.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade any user information to third parties. All API interactions are conducted strictly between our local server and the respective official platform APIs over encrypted HTTPS channels.

4. Data Retention and Security

Access credentials and tokens are stored in private, restricted-access configuration files with strict permissions. Users may revoke access tokens at any time directly through their TikTok or Google account security settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please reach out via GitHub: github.com/NilSerra.
